Our Story

Radiance Studio was founded in 2015 in Johannesburg by a small group of passionate hairstylists who shared one simple belief: that great hair care should make people feel confident, cared for, and truly seen. What started as a single-chair salon has grown into a full-service hair studio, trusted by clients across the city for everything from everyday braids to special-occasion styling. Over the years we've stayed true to our roots — personal attention, honest advice, and a genuine love for the craft of hairdressing.

Our Mission

To provide every client with expert hair care in a welcoming environment, using quality products and techniques that protect the health of their hair while helping them look and feel radiant.

Our Vision

To be Johannesburg's most trusted hair studio, recognised for our skill, creativity, and the genuine relationships we build with every client who walks through our doors.
